1. Your earning

$ /hr
Your gross hourly rate. If salaried, divide annual salary by hours worked per year.
%
Combined federal, state, and payroll tax. Take-home pay divided by gross pay gives a good estimate.
$ /mo
Rent, utilities, insurance, food, transport. What you pay before any discretionary spending. This unlocks the discretionary framing.
hr/yr
2000 hours is a standard full-time year (40 hours * 50 weeks). Adjust for part-time, overtime, or teaching schedules.

2. Time framing

Discretionary is the most honest: post-tax income minus fixed expenses. It's the unit you actually trade when you buy something.

4. Reference units

Nobody really feels "$70." Define something meaningful and see prices in units of it: a gym membership, a week of groceries, a tank of gas, whatever you spend on regularly.

5. Marketplace and currency

The extension auto-detects Amazon's marketplace and currency symbol from the page. Your wage is assumed to be in the currency of whichever Amazon site you're browsing.

¥ amazon.co.jp

If you cross between marketplaces regularly, update your wage to the local currency before browsing. The extension does not convert between currencies.
