Amazon Time Cost

Settings

Wage and tax

Used to translate prices into hours of work. Tax rate enables the post-tax framings.

Personal expenses

Optional. Required for the "discretionary hours" and "days of leisure" framings. Leave blank to keep those framings disabled.

Display framing

What the time badge shows next to each price. Secondary appears on hover.

Compound opportunity cost

Show what the dollar amount would become if invested instead.

Other features

Donation link

A small support link shows in the footer below if a URL is set. Buy Me a Coffee, GitHub Sponsors, and Ko-fi all work.
